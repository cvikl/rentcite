Section 1. As used in this chapter, the following words shall, unless the context clearly
requires otherwise, have the following meanings:

8

“Coordinate”, with respect to a service provider, the:

9

(i) collecting of historical or contemporaneous actual rent prices, price changes, supply

10

levels, occupancy rates or lease or rental contract termination and renewal dates of residential

11

dwelling units;

(ii) analyzing or processing of the information described in clause (i), including with the
use of a system, software or process that uses computation; and
(iii) recommending of rental prices, lease terms, occupancy levels, or other commercial

15

16

“Person”, any natural person, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, firm or
association.
“Predispute arbitration agreement”, an agreement to arbitrate a dispute that has not yet
arisen at the time of the making of the agreement.

21

“Predispute joint-action waiver”, an agreement, whether or not part of a predispute

22

arbitration agreement, that would prohibit or waive the right of one of the parties to participate in

23

a joint, class or collective action in a judicial, arbitral, administrative or other forum, concerning

24

a dispute that has not yet arisen at the time of the making of the agreement.

25

“Real estate lessor”, any individual, corporation, partnership, association, joint-stock

26

company, trust or unincorporated organization that owns real property and leases such property

27

or any portion thereof in the form of a residential dwelling unit.

28

“Residential dwelling unit”, any house, apartment, accessory unit or other unit intended

29

to be used as a person’s primary residence; provided, that “residential dwelling unit” shall not

30

include inpatient medical care, licensed long-term care and detention or correctional facilities.

31

“Service provider”, any person that performs a coordination function for any real estate

32

lessor or rentor.

Section 2. (a) No real estate lessor, or any agent or subcontractor thereof, shall subscribe

34

to, contract with or otherwise exchange anything of value in return for the services of a service

35

provider.

36
37
38

(b) No service provider shall facilitate an agreement to not compete among real estate
lessors with respect to residential dwelling units.
Section 3. (a) A violation of this section shall also constitute an unfair method of

39

competition, and a violation of the provisions of chapter 93 and chapter 93A. Nothing in this

40

section shall impair or limit the applicability of either chapter or the antitrust laws, generally.

41

(b) Pursuant to a motion by the plaintiff promptly made, the court may award simple

42

interest on actual damages sustained by the plaintiff for the period beginning on the date of

43

service of the pleading of the plaintiff setting forth a claim under this chapter and ending on the

44

date of judgment, or for any shorter period therein.

45

(c) At the election of the person alleging conduct constituting a violation of this section,

46

or the named representative of a class or in a collective action alleging such conduct, no

47

predispute arbitration agreement or predispute joint-action waiver shall be valid or enforceable

48

with respect to a case which is filed under federal, tribal or state or local law and relates to a

49

violation of this chapter.

50

(d) In the case of any successful action to enforce liability pursuant to this chapter, the

51

costs of the action together with reasonable attorney fees, expert fees and the costs of

52

investigation as determined by the court shall be awarded to plaintiffs. Costs and attorney fees,

53

54
